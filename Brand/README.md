# Goku brand artwork

`GokuAppIconSource.png` is the canonical source for the app icon and was supplied by the repository owner on 2026-08-16. `Scripts/generate_goku_branding.py` deterministically produces the checked-in 1024×1024 icon assets and Goku wordmark layers.

The character artwork is recognizable as Goku from Dragon Ball and may be subject to third-party copyright or trademark rights. Inclusion in this fork does not itself establish permission for public commercial distribution; confirm those rights before a public App Store release.
